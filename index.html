How it works

Three steps from search to session

1. Find

Filter tutors by skill, block or hostel, and see how many sessions they have done.

2. Book

Send a request with your preferred time. Agree on a free session, a fee or a skill swap.

3. Learn

Meet at the Central Library or a block common room, learn for an hour and share what you know back.
