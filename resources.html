Frequently asked questions

Click a question to open the answer.

Is SkillSwap free?

Joining is free. Sessions can be free, a skill swap, or paid up to Rs 200 per hour.

How do I book a session?

Open the Skills page, pick a tutor, press Book Session and send your preferred time.

How do I meet safely?

Meet in public campus places during the day and tell a friend where you are going.

How do skill swaps work?

You teach the tutor something you know, and they teach you something in return.

How do I become a tutor?

Fill the Become a Tutor form on the Skills page with your skill and availability.

What if a session goes badly?

Report it through the Contact page and the student team will follow up.

Safety rules for campus meet-ups

  1. Meet in public campus locations.
  2. Keep first sessions during the day, between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
  3. Check the tutor's block or hostel badge.
  4. Tell a hostel-mate or classmate where you are going.
  5. Share a registration number, never bank or card details.
  6. Report uncomfortable behaviour through the Contact page.
